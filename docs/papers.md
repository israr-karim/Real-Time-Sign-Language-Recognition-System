Project manuscripts and papers used as sources need distinct attribution. Dataset and backbone references are already recorded in [references.bib](references.bib).

The supplied full thesis report is now identified in the [thesis record](thesis.md), with all authors, its institutional repository link, and implementation-scope notes. It is distinct from a peer-reviewed journal or conference paper.

The project author confirmed on **2026-11-05** that the separate review manuscript and IEEE-formatted manuscript are **unpublished drafts submitted to the university**. They have not been represented here as peer-reviewed or IEEE publications. Their titles, complete author lists, files, and versions will be indexed after those manuscripts are supplied and reviewed against the documented experimental limitations.

## Papers authored as part of this project

For each manuscript, record its title, all authors in the agreed order, date, relationship to the code, and exact status: draft, submitted, accepted, or published. Include a venue, DOI, or acceptance claim only when verified. A manuscript written in an IEEE template should be labelled as a draft or submitted manuscript until its actual status changes.

Results discussed in a manuscript should be accompanied by the repository's current evaluation limitations. Multiple documents describing the same project should explain their relationship.

## If a manuscript is later published by IEEE

Prefer a DOI, IEEE Xplore link, or an authorized preprint link. IEEE's [conference policy](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/post-publication-policies/) and [journal policy](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/post-publication-policies/) distinguish submitted manuscripts, accepted manuscripts, and publisher versions. For standard-copyright work, a publisher PDF generally cannot be posted online; accepted-manuscript posting has location and notice requirements. Open-access permissions depend on the applicable license. Do not assume that a GitHub repository is an authorized posting location for every version.

Confirm the specific paper's terms before placing its PDF here. Keep all author credits and required notices intact.

## Papers used as sources

Add a citation and official link to the bibliography. Identify them as related work, dataset references, or model references. Full-text uploads should have an established redistribution basis. A source paper should not be presented as a publication authored by this project's contributors.
